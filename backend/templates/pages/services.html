Services

Six ways we help organizations carry risk

Most engagements draw on more than one of these. We'll scope the combination that matches what you're actually facing, not a fixed package.

01

Enterprise risk assessment

A full inventory of operational, financial, regulatory, and reputational exposure, scored by likelihood and consequence and mapped against what would actually happen to your organisation if each risk materialised.

Good for: boards and leadership teams who need an honest baseline before setting strategy or raising capital.

02

Business continuity & resilience planning

Continuity plans built around Nepal's real disruption patterns — seismic events, monsoon flooding, landslides, and transport corridor closures — with defined recovery steps and responsibility owners.

Good for: organizations with physical operations, branch networks, or supply chains that can't afford an undefined recovery process.

03

Regulatory & compliance advisory

Ongoing guidance on Nepal Rastra Bank directives, Insurance Authority requirements, and sector-specific regulation, translated into practical changes to policy, reporting, and internal controls.

Good for: financial institutions, cooperatives, and regulated sectors managing frequent regulatory change.

04

Insurance & risk transfer advisory

Independent review of what's worth insuring, what's better retained, and how existing policies actually perform against your risk profile — without a commission tied to any policy we recommend.

Good for: organizations renewing cover, entering new markets, or unsure whether current policies actually protect them.

05

Financial & credit risk analysis

Portfolio concentration, counterparty exposure, and credit risk reviews for lenders and finance companies, with stress-testing against currency movement and sector downturns.

Good for: banks, cooperatives, microfinance institutions, and lenders managing growing loan books.

06

Training & risk culture

Practical workshops for leadership and operational teams that move risk thinking out of a compliance binder and into how day-to-day decisions actually get made.

Good for: organizations that have a risk policy on paper but want it reflected in daily practice.

Not sure which of these fits?

Most clients don't arrive knowing exactly what they need. Describe the decision or the disruption you're facing, and we'll recommend a starting point.
